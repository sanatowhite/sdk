# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-06_

This is placeholder content for the App Template. **Replace this file with your own privacy policy before shipping to real users.**

## What this template collects (if you enable it)

- **Diagnostics**: crash reports, ANR signals, startup/frame timing, and basic memory usage, if you enable the diagnostics toggle in Settings.
- **Network requests**: timing metadata (route, method, status, duration) for requests made through this app's networking layer.

No diagnostics are collected before you accept this policy, and you can turn diagnostics off at any time from Settings.

## What this template does not do

- No account creation, no authentication.
- No advertising identifiers.
- No data is sold to third parties.

## Your choices

You can disable diagnostics sharing at any time in **Settings > Privacy**.
